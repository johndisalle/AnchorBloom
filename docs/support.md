## Frequently Asked Questions

### Account

**How do I create an account?**
You can sign up with your email address or use Sign in with Apple for a quick, private setup.

**How do I reset my password?**
On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot Password" and enter your email. You'll receive a reset link.

**How do I delete my account?**
Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all associated data. This action cannot be undone.

### Subscription

**What's included in the free version?**
Free members get daily morning and evening devotionals, basic progress tracking (streaks and badges), guided journeys (free tier), and the ability to browse and view Sister Circles.

**What does Premium include?**
Premium unlocks all 30-day guided journeys, full Sister Circle participation (post, comment, react), deeper devotional prompts, detailed growth insights and weekly reports, personalized scripture reminders throughout the day, custom spiritual goals, and an ad-free experience.

**How much does Premium cost?**
- Monthly: $6.99/month
- Yearly: $59.99/year (save 28%)

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Subscriptions are managed through Apple. Go to your iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > Anchor & Bloom > Cancel Subscription. Your premium features will remain active until the end of your billing period.

**How do I restore my purchase?**
Go to Settings > Subscription > Restore Purchases within the App.

### Sister Circles

**What are Sister Circles?**
Sister Circles are small community groups where women can share encouragement, prayer requests, praise reports, and testimonies in a safe, faith-centered space.

**How do I join a circle?**
Tap "Join Circle" on the Circles tab and enter the invite code shared by the circle's creator. You can also browse and join public circles.

**How do I create a circle?**
Tap "Create Circle" on the Circles tab. Free members can create up to 2 circles. Premium members can create unlimited circles.

**How do I report inappropriate content?**
Tap the three-dot menu (...) on any post or comment and select "Report." Choose a reason and optionally add details. Our team reviews all reports.

**How do I block a user?**
Tap the three-dot menu (...) on any post or comment from that user and select "Block." You can manage blocked users in Settings > Privacy & Safety > Blocked Users.

### Daily Devotionals

**When should I do the Morning Anchor?**
The Morning Anchor is designed for your morning quiet time. It helps you identify lies or struggles and anchor yourself in God's truth for the day.

**When should I do the Evening Bloom?**
The Evening Bloom is an evening reflection. It helps you recognize how God worked through you during the day and celebrate the roles you fulfilled.

**What is the Drift Log?**
The Drift Log helps you notice moments when you drifted from peace — comparison, fear, people-pleasing, etc. — and turn those moments into prayers.

### Technical Issues

**The app is running slowly.**
Try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, check that your iOS is updated to version 17 or later.

**I'm not receiving notifications.**
Make sure notifications are enabled in both the App (Settings > Daily Reminders) and your iPhone Settings (Settings > Notifications > Anchor & Bloom).

**Sign in with Apple isn't working.**
Ensure you have a stable internet connection. If the issue persists, try signing in with email instead, or restart the app and try again.
