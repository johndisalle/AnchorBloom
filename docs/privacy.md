# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: March 26, 2026**

Anchor & Bloom ("the App", "we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your information when you use our App.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Account Information**: Name, email address, and authentication credentials when you create an account (including via Sign in with Apple).
- **Profile Information**: Display name and profile preferences.
- **Devotional Content**: Morning reflections, evening reflections, drift log entries, and spiritual journal content you create within the App.
- **Community Content**: Posts, comments, and reactions you share in Sister Circles.
- **Preferences**: Notification settings, reminder times, appearance preferences, and subscription choices.

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Usage Data**: Features used, pages visited, and interaction patterns within the App to improve user experience.
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version, and app version for compatibility and troubleshooting.
- **Crash Data**: Anonymous crash reports to help us fix bugs and improve stability.

### Information from Third Parties
- **Apple Sign In**: If you use Sign in with Apple, we receive your name and email (or a relay email if you choose to hide it).
- **Payment Information**: Subscription purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Provide and maintain the App and its features
- Create and manage your account
- Process your subscription and provide premium features
- Send daily reminder notifications (if enabled)
- Display your content within community features (Sister Circles)
- Track your spiritual growth progress (streaks, badges, journey progress)
- Improve the App through anonymous usage analytics
- Respond to your support requests
- Enforce our Terms & Conditions and community guidelines

## 3. How We Store Your Information

- **Cloud Storage**: Your account data, devotional entries, and community content are stored securely in Google Firebase (Firestore), hosted in the United States.
- **Local Storage**: App preferences and settings may be stored locally on your device.
- **Encryption**: Data is encrypted in transit using TLS/SSL and at rest using Firebase's built-in encryption.

## 4. How We Share Your Information

We do **not** sell your personal information. We share your information only in these limited circumstances:

- **Community Features**: Posts, comments, and your display name are visible to other members of your Sister Circles. Your devotional reflections and journal entries are private and never shared.
- **Service Providers**: We use Google Firebase for backend services. Firebase processes data in accordance with Google's privacy policies.
- **Legal Requirements**: We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or government request.
- **Safety**: We may share information to protect the safety of our users, investigate violations of our Terms, or respond to emergencies.

## 5. Your Privacy Choices

### What's Private (Only You Can See)
- Morning Anchor reflections
- Evening Bloom reflections
- Drift log entries
- Bookmarked verses
- Spiritual goals
- Progress statistics and streaks

### What's Shared (Visible to Circle Members)
- Your display name
- Posts and comments in Sister Circles
- Reactions (likes) on community content

### Your Controls
- **Notifications**: Enable or disable all notifications, and customize reminder times in Settings.
- **Block Users**: Block any user to prevent them from seeing your community content and vice versa.
- **Report Content**: Report inappropriate content for review.
- **Delete Account**: Delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings > Account > Delete Account.

## 6. Data Retention

- **Active Accounts**: We retain your data for as long as your account is active.
- **Deleted Accounts**: When you delete your account, we delete your personal data, devotional entries, and community content. Some anonymized data may be retained for analytics purposes.
- **Community Content**: Posts and comments you made in circles may be deleted upon account deletion.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Anchor & Bloom is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

## 8. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:
- **Firebase Authentication**: For account creation and sign-in
- **Cloud Firestore**: For data storage
- **Firebase Analytics**: For anonymous usage analytics
- **Apple StoreKit**: For subscription management

Each service has its own privacy policy governing its data practices.

## 9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including:
- Encrypted data transmission (TLS/SSL)
- Encrypted data storage
- Firebase security rules restricting data access
- Authentication required for all data operations

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. If you become aware of a security vulnerability, please contact us immediately.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the App or by email. The "Last Updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

## 11. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your data
- Export your data
- Opt out of certain data processing

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us.
